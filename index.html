Features

Everything required for automated climate coverage

🌦️Real-time telemetry

Live AQI, PM2.5, ozone, temperature and humidity for any city from Open-Meteo.

🍃Computer vision crop scan

HSV segmentation measures leaf damage in your browser, with overlay and mask views.

📉Parametric loss model

Threshold rules turn damage, humidity and ozone into a yield-loss estimate and a trust score.

⚡Instant eligibility trigger

The payout rule runs automatically, so there is no claim paperwork to file.

🔗Immutable proof

SHA-256 chained blocks anchored on Polygon Amoy, with hash verification and a tamper test.

🌐Bilingual Urdu RTL mode

One-click English and Urdu interface for regional accessibility.
